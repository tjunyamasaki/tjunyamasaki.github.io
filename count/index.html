Count

Watch the stage. Count what appears. First correct wins a race; timed flashes can have many winners.

How to play
  • 2–15 players. Host picks rounds, Easy / Normal / Hard, and Mix or one mini-game.
  • Mix is random each round at that difficulty. One mini-game starts easy and gets harder.
  • Each round is a short counting scene. One guess each.
  • Timed: the picture hides, then everyone answers. All correct scores count.
  • Race: guess while things move. First correct score wins the round.
  • Highest score after the last round wins. Ties share the win.
