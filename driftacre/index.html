WHILE YOU WERE AWAY

The island kept growing.

    A QUIET MOMENT

    Island settings

    Tend Tap or drag across tiles: harvest ripe crops, plant your chosen seed on empty ground, and tap a growing sprout once to help it along.

    Island heart Every harvest feeds it. When it blooms you get a land sprout — use Land to grow a new tile anywhere on the edge.

    Sproutlings Build Sprout Huts. Each one houses a little helper who harvests, replants and tends — even while you are away.

    Sky visitors Tap the sky whale, gift balloons and falling stars.

    Keys WASD / arrows pan · Q E rotate · scroll or + − zoom · 1–9 pick seed · B build · L land · Esc back. Right-drag also pans. On touch: drag empty sky to pan, pinch to zoom.
