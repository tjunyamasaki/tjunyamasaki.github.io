Trick-taking

Fodinha

Bid exactly the tricks you will take. Miss, and you lose lives. Last player standing wins.

How to play
  • 3–6 players. Everyone starts with 5 lives.
  • 40-card deck, Truco ranking (4 low … 3 high).
  • A vira is turned; the next rank are manilhas (clubs strongest).
  • Bid the exact number of tricks you will take. Dealer is hooked: bids cannot sum to the hand size.
  • Follow suit if you can. Manilhas beat everything; among them clubs > hearts > spades > diamonds.
  • Lose lives equal to how far you missed. Last with lives wins.
