Conway’s Game of Life

Generation 0 · Population 0 · Step to see births and deaths

Click or drag to paint. Shift erases. Right-drag or Pan to move. Wheel zooms. Pick a pattern, then click to stamp (R rotate, F flip).

Patterns

Stamp famous objects, then compose them. A glider is a bit; a gun is a clock; an eater is a sink.

Rules (B3/S23)

A dead cell is born with exactly 3 live neighbors. A live cell survives with 2 or 3. Otherwise it dies. After each step, births are green and deaths are red. Edges are dead (no wrap) so guns and circuits can fire into empty space.

Cells to bits

A 6-step tour

Still life → oscillator → glider → gun → eater → collision. Each step loads a pattern; Play or Step to watch.
