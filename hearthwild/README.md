# Hearthwild

A little home above the clouds. Wander a seeded floating island, gather wood, stone, copper, berries, carrots and potatoes, plant a garden, smelt ingots, and keep one island saved in this browser.

- On the title, choose a **name**, a **seed**, and **Moonlit** or **Ember**. Leave the seed blank for a surprise. **Continue** restores the saved island; it does not grant the starting kit again.
- You begin with a **stone axe** and **stone pick**, and no crop seeds. The action dock has **Gather**, **Hoe**, and **Seeds**. Default is Gather: tap a tree, outcrop or wild crop, walk close, then **hold Gather** (or **E** / **Space**). The keeper chops, mines, reaches or pulls in place. Wild carrots and potatoes return produce, seeds and fiber. Pause, moving, or walking out of range cancels with no reward. Reduced motion uses a still working pose.
- **Bag** (or **I**) lists every count. Ordinary materials have no bag limit. Gold rings mark protected supply that cannot be removed. Stumps and unprotected plants can be cleared. Hand-craft a **hoe** from **5 wood + 2 stone** once; pulling wild or garden crops does not need it. Choose **Hoe** to hold it in both hands even while idle. Tap grass to aim: an irregular earth outline and a small hoe or reject icon mark the spot, with Till or the rejection reason in the HUD. Hold **Till** / **E**: the keeper lifts the hoe and scrapes the earth. At the 70% scrape, brown earth chips and a few dry grass flecks kick up; earth appears only when the 0.9s action finishes. Decorative grass whose roots sit in that earth hides; smoothing restores it. Move or release to cancel with no soil.
- **Build** (or **B**) opens a catalog of floor, path, workbench and furnace. Ghosts, rotation and moving spend nothing. **Confirm** pays once; **Dismantle** returns the original construction materials. New earth comes from tilling grass with a hoe, not from Build. A furnace needs a workbench within five paces. Escape cancels a ghost before pause. Pinch zooms and never places.
- **Seeds** on the dock shows carrot and potato counts. Pick a species, tap empty earth, and hold **Plant**. The keeper kneels, the near hand drops two or three seed specks, then pats the earth; the hoe stays put away. Cancel before 0.65s spends nothing and the specks vanish. There is no plot sheet, Soil catalog, or crop-recipe list: farming stays on Gather / Hoe / Seeds. Plant saplings from **Bag**. Harvest ready plots in Gather without a hoe: the keeper bends, grips the leaves, and pulls the root; canceling restores the plant and removes the lifted clone. **Uproot** is a shorter 0.6s pull that returns one seed and no produce; a small seed cue appears at the end, not a lifted harvest. Empty earth can be **smoothed** back to grass with a short hoe rake (0.6s); grass returns when the action finishes. With **Sound** on, tilling makes a quiet earth thud at the scrape, planting a seed rustle, and harvest or uproot a soft root pull; Sound off, Pause, and reduced motion stay silent for those contact ticks. Leftover moonflowers can still become fiber in Bag. Growth pauses in Bag, stations and Pause.
- Select a workbench or furnace and **Use** (or **E**). Station panels pause the island; close them to resume smelting even if you walk away. Smelt **2 ore + 1 wood** into an ingot, then upgrade a copper axe or pick once. Stone tools stay owned. Queue at most ten unfinished batches; only waiting batches refund.
- **Save** keeps this island in this browser. Pause, harvests, builds, and a short walk also autosave. Starting a new island asks first and does not replace the saved slot until the new island writes.
- Move with **WASD**, **arrow keys**, or **tap the ground**. **Pinch to zoom**, or use **+ / −**. **Escape** pauses. **Return to clearing** brings the keeper back to the meadow.
- **Inspect** frames the keeper at 9×; wheel, pinch or **+ / −** zooms up to **14×**. **Right-drag** orbits; **Shift + right-drag** or **middle-drag** pans. **Camera** exposes orbit/tilt/pan buttons and touch drag modes; return to **Walk / select** to interact. Two fingers pan and pinch. Movement keys stay relative to the view. Tap the zoom number to reset the view; **Center on keeper** clears panning. Nearby tree crowns become translucent at close zoom so hands and tools stay visible.

Cottages, furniture and wildlife controls are not in this walk yet.

Requires JavaScript and WebGL2. Three.js is included locally under its MIT license in `vendor/LICENSE`.
