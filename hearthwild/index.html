TAKE YOUR TIME

A moment of quiet.

WanderWASD, arrow keys, or tap the ground. Walk around trees, rocks and workbenches; you cannot fall. Tap a plant, outcrop or placed piece to see its name, then walk to a nearby clearing.

GatherThe dock has Gather, Hoe, and Seeds. Default is Gather: chop, mine, pick, and pull mature wild or garden crops with empty hands. Hold the action button or E / Space while in reach. An immature garden crop shows its growth and will not uproot from Harvest. Pause, blur, moving, or walking out of range cancels with no reward. Inventory (I) pauses the island. Protected gold-ring patches cannot be removed. Reduced motion keeps a still pose and the usual progress ring.

BuildB or Build opens the catalog. Choosing a piece shows a ghost; the island keeps moving. Tap a cell, R or Rotate turns it, Confirm pays once. Soil is not in Build: till grass with a hoe. A furnace needs a workbench within five paces. Escape cancels the ghost first. Pinch zooms and never places.

GardenNew islands start with no crop seeds: pull wild orange carrots and yellowish potatoes to find them. Craft a hoe (5 wood + 2 stone) in Bag, choose Hoe to hold it, and tap grass. An irregular earth outline and a hoe or reject icon show where Till will land; the HUD names Till or the reason it will not work. Hold Till. The scrape kicks up brown earth chips and a few dry grass flecks; earth still appears only when Till finishes. Seeds shows carrot and potato counts; pick one, tap empty earth, hold Plant. Planting kneels, drops a few seed specks, and pats the earth; canceling before it finishes spends nothing and the specks vanish. Harvest a ready garden crop in Gather: the keeper bends, grips the leaves, and pulls the root up; canceling restores the plant. Uproot is a shorter 0.6s pull on a garden crop: one seed returns, no produce, and a small seed cue at the end. Smooth earth is a short hoe rake on empty earth; grass returns when it finishes. With Sound on, tilling thuds quietly at the scrape, planting rustles, and harvest or uproot makes a soft root pull; Sound off, Pause, and reduced motion keep those contact ticks silent. Plant saplings from Bag. There is no plot sheet or Soil catalog. Growth pauses in Bag, stations, Pause and hidden tabs.

StationsSelect a workbench or furnace, then Use or E. Station panels pause the island, like Bag. Close them to resume smelting even if you walk away. Activity still shows in the HUD. Reach is two paces with a clear approach. Recipes use the bag.

LookInspect gives a close view of the keeper and tools. Pause or the title screen can switch between the hooded cloak and the garden-brim look. Wheel, pinch or + / − zoom up to 14×. Right-drag orbits; Shift + right-drag or middle-drag pans. On touch, use Camera → Orbit / Pan or two-finger pan/pinch; return to Walk / select to interact. WASD stays relative to the camera. Tap the zoom number to reset the whole view.

PauseEscape or Pause stops time and cancels gathering. Switching tabs does the same. Bag and station panels also freeze timers. Build preview is not a pause menu.
