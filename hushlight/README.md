# Hushlight

A little night garden, floating somewhere quiet. Choose **Three sleeping shrines** for a short journey, or **Endless offerings** to keep gathering lights for the altar in the middle.

Choose the cool **Moonlit garden** or the darker, brown-and-red **Ember night**. You can also change the atmosphere while paused.

- Move with **WASD**, **arrow keys**, or **tap the ground**.
- **Pinch to zoom** on mobile, or use **+ / −** (or the mouse wheel). Tap the zoom number to see the whole island. At close zoom the camera follows you.
- Walk near a light to invite it along. Up to three will follow.
- Stand beside a sleeping crystal with three lights, then **hold Space, E, or the kindle button**.
- In endless mode, offer three lights at the **middle altar**. The lights return to the garden, and each offering sends lanterns into the sky.
- **Escape** pauses and shows the controls. Sound is optional.

There is no timer to beat, and you cannot fall. After the garden wakes, stay awhile or begin another night.

To change game mode, pause and choose **Start a different garden**. This begins a fresh run.

Requires JavaScript and WebGL2. Three.js is included locally under its MIT license in `vendor/LICENSE`.
