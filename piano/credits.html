Pocket Piano uses recordings of a Yamaha C5 grand from Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm, shared under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported license.

These 21 MP3 recordings come from the Tone.js audio collection. The recordings are included unchanged. While you play, nearby notes are pitch-shifted, softly filtered, panned, and given a little room reverb.

On a phone, use a few fingers to play chords or slide between keys. Touch nearer the bottom of a key for a slightly stronger note. Turn on Sustain to let notes ring after you lift your fingers.

On a computer, use A, W, S, E, D, F, T, G, Y, H, U, J, K for a chromatic octave. O, L, P, semicolon, and apostrophe continue into the next octave on wider screens. Z and X move down and up an octave. Hold Space for the sustain pedal, or press Escape to release everything. You can also Tab to a piano key and hold Enter or Space to play it.

The short demo melody was created for this piano. All sound runs in your browser.
