# Salamander Grand Piano

Recordings by **Alexander Holm**, of a Yamaha C5 grand piano.

- Original collection: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3

The 21 MP3 files in this directory are an unmodified subset of the Tone.js
distribution, C2 through C7 in minor thirds. The upstream README is preserved
in `UPSTREAM-README.txt`. Playback applies pitch shifting, a velocity-dependent
low-pass filter, stereo panning, gain envelopes, compression, and room reverb.

Attribution is also linked in the piano's footer under “About the sound.”
