Climbing / shedding

Presidente

Empty your hand first to become Presidente. Last out is Bobo and pays tax next hand. First to 11 points wins.

How to play
  • 2–10 players. French 52-card deck (two decks if 6+).
  • Rank: 3 is weakest, 2 is strongest (then A, K, Q…).
  • Lead any number of the same rank. Follow with the same count, strictly higher.
  • Pass and you are out of that pile. When everyone still in has passed, the last player to lay cards leads again.
  • A 2 (matching the count) closes the pile; that player leads again.
  • First out is Presidente (+2), Vice (+1 if 4+ players), last is Bobo. First to 11 points wins.
  • Tax next hand: 5 or fewer players — last place gives 1 highest card to first, who returns any 1. More than 5 — last gives 2 to first, second-to-last gives 1 to second; each returns the same count.
  • First hand: the player with 3♠ leads. Later hands: Presidente leads after tax.
