# Spin Lab

A hands-on table tennis playground. Open [Spin Lab](./) and explore the five experiments.

- **Spin:** swipe the ball to add rotation, or tap the X/Y/Z controls. Add a second component and watch the axis tilt.
- **Return:** try incoming-spin presets and shape the racket stroke. Drag the racket in the arena, or open **Adjust** for the direction arrow and contact dot.
- **Serve:** drag the toss handle upward. Adjust the contact height, brush, face and speed, then follow the ball from release to bounce.
- **Bounce:** launch the same shot with different spin. The dashed reference starts without spin. Try the side and top cameras.
- **Compare:** play two experiments on the same clock. Racket impacts line up, including high and low tosses.

Drag empty space to orbit. Pinch to zoom. The camera menu includes player, side, top, ball and contact views. On a keyboard, use arrow keys to orbit, plus/minus to zoom, and Space to pause. The stroke arrow, contact dot, toss height and all other controls also support keyboard input.

Scrub the timeline to examine a moment. Tap the event markers to jump directly to contact. Use **Why?** to inspect the vectors, replay impact slowly, or expand the numbers. **Discover** offers five short experiments.

The lime arrow is angular velocity; cyan shows linear velocity, purple shows racket motion, and orange shows friction. Labels explain each quantity. Component arrows and the resultant share a common display scale.

Surface rotation is slowed by default so the markings stay readable. The flight still uses the actual RPM. Disable readable rotation in settings to see the physical angular speed (high spin can alias on a screen).

Physics are simplified for visualization and learning. The ball can hit the net or miss the table when you change a stroke. Pure vertical sidespin bends flight but does not directly produce sideways friction at the bottom of an ideal sphere. Tilt the spin axis to explore that effect.

No account, sound, or connection to other players is needed. Sound is optional and starts muted.
