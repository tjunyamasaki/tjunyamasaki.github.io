    A TILE-LAYING GAME

    Lay the land.
    Watch it live.

    Draw a tile, fit it to the kingdom, and send your followers to claim towns, roads, abbeys and meadows. Every tile you lay grows walls, farms and forests, and fills with people and sheep.

    2–5 seats · mix people at this screen with bots

    PAUSED

    How to play

    Lay a tile.Every edge must match its neighbours: town to town, road to road, meadow to meadow. Tap a glowing spot, turn the tile with ↻ (or R, or right-click), then press Place.

    Send a followeronto the tile you just laid, if no one already holds that town, road, abbey or meadow. You have seven.

    Finish features to score.Town: 2 per tile and 2 per blue shield. Road: 1 per tile. Abbey: 9 once surrounded. Followers come home when their feature is finished. Most followers takes the points; ties share them.

    At the endunfinished towns score 1 per tile and shield, roads 1 per tile, abbeys 1 plus each neighbour, and meadow farmers 3 for each finished town their meadow touches.

    Look around.Drag to move · right-drag, Q/E or two fingers to turn and tilt · scroll or pinch to zoom · V changes the view · T changes the sky.
